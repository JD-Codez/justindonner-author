Author & Advocate

Meet Justin Donner

Originally from County Donegal roots, Justin Donner is an author, father, criminologist, and financial agent. Following years of undiagnosed bipolar disorder and severe substance abuse, Justin entered recovery in 2012.

After journeys from Bellingham Bay to the barns of North Dakota and the beaches of Oceanside, California, his days are now centered on self-discipline, fatherhood, and helping others conquer their own battles.

Justin writes openly on controversial realities—substance abuse, psychiatric illness, toxic masculinity, and domestic violence prevention—urging society to move past finger-pointing toward honest understanding and healing. He is also the author of Ninja Cat: Whiskers of Choice.
